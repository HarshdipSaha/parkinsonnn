# PPMI DaT-SPECT: what we would get and what the DUA allows

Wayfinder research ticket, 2026-09-07. All claims below come from public PPMI/LONI documents, the DrivenData rules and forum, and papers that describe the PPMI image files; nothing here touches challenge test data. Where a fact could only be confirmed after an IDA login, it is marked **verify after login**.

## TL;DR

- PPMI's downloadable DaT-SPECT product is the core-lab **"Reconstructed DaTSCAN"**: HOSEM-reconstructed, Chang-attenuation-corrected, 6 mm Gaussian-smoothed, PMOD-warped to an MNI ioflupane template, delivered as one multi-frame NM DICOM per scan on a **91 x 109 x 91, 2 mm** grid (the MNI152 2 mm box). Per-scan SBR tables and a per-scan **positive/negative visual read** table ship alongside. [8][12][13][22][23][25]
- Roughly 1.0-1.1k subjects with DaT imaging are visible in the literature/user guide (PD ~800-1,100, HC ~200-300, SWEDD ~60-80, prodromal several hundred, plus PPMI Clinical screening of "approximately 6,000"); exact scan counts need an IDA login. [8][13][15][28]
- The DUA (v5.0, April 2026) allows training models on PPMI ("both permitted and encouraged") but forbids uploading data to third-party platforms that retain user content (Kaggle falls under this), forbids sharing any participant-level data (including PATNO/scan lists) with anyone not registered with PPMI, and lets trained weights be shared only "through MJFF-approved methods". [1]
- DrivenData's organiser ruled on 2026-08-05 that "models trained on this data wouldn't be eligible for prizes" because "competition use doesn't fall under those purposes" and winners must be MIT-licensed. Using PPMI in any submission is outside the DUA's stated purpose. [16][19]
- Access review: "within one week of receipt" for the standard tiers; prodromal DaTscan results are Tier 3 (DAC review "within 30 days"). With the deadline on 2026-09-16, PPMI is a research-side asset, not a competition asset. [2][3][4]

## 1. Image products

| Product (IDA / download name) | What it is | Format | Grid / space | Count (best public evidence) | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw projection data | Site acquisition: 128 x 128 matrix, 120 projections (60 per detector, 3 deg steps), 30 s/projection, 20% window at 159 keV, LEHR collimators, zoom 1.23; submitted to the core lab as "Raw Projection SPECT Files in DICOM Format" | DICOM NM (projections) | Native camera space | Every scan has one; **verify after login** whether IDA exposes them (an "Original" series) | [9][10][11] |
| Site reconstruction | Each centre reconstructs "following their standard protocol for clinical brain SPECT" and submits "Reconstructed SPECT Files in DICOM Format" | DICOM NM | Native site space (like the challenge scans) | Same as above; **verify after login** | [10][11] |
| **Reconstructed DaTSCAN** (core lab, pre-processed) | HERMES "iterative (HOSEM) reconstruction", Chang ellipse attenuation correction with a site-specific mu from phantom data, "six-millimeter Gaussian Smoothing filter", PMOD normalisation "to a SPECT ioflupane reference template in standard Montreal Neurologic Institute (MNI) space"; follow-ups are rigidly co-registered to the screening scan and pushed through the screening transform | One multi-frame DICOM per scan: Modality NM, SeriesDescription "Reconstructed DaTSCAN", Rows 109, Columns 91, NumberOfFrames 91, PixelSpacing 2.0/2.0, SliceThickness 2.0, IOP [1,0,0,0,1,0], IPP [-90,-90,-74], RescaleSlope ~0.0041 (header dump of one file [22]); some users also report NIfTI export [24] | 91 x 109 x 91 at 2 mm, MNI (LPS DICOM convention) | Baseline: 449 PD + 210 HC (2019-2021 pulls); 816 PD + 212 HC as of 2023-03-24 (reported in [28]); PD follow-ups at ~1, 2, 4, 5 y, most HC single scan | [12][13][22][23][24][25][28] |
| DaTscan Analysis (CSV) | SBR "of left and right caudate, putamen and anterior putamen regions with respect to the occipital lobe"; 8 hottest striatal slices summed; SBR = (ROI - occipital)/occipital | CSV keyed PATNO + EVENT_ID | n/a | One row per analysed scan | [8][12] |
| DaTscan Visual Interpretation Results (CSV) | "Summary result of DaTscan visual interpretation, i.e., positive / negative"; 2 reviewers, 3rd for discordance, per DaTscan USPI criteria, read in PMOD | CSV | n/a | Per read scan | [8][12] |
| DaTscan Imaging / DaTscan Metadata (CSV) | Scan date, location, time post-injection, injected dose | CSV | n/a | Per scan | [8] |

Notes. (a) The 91 x 109 x 91 / 2 mm grid is exactly the size of our 2 mm study-template frame if that frame is the MNI152 2 mm box; the DICOM origin (-90, -90, -74) in LPS matches the FSL MNI152 2 mm bounding box up to a 2 mm z-offset (check on the first file). (b) Intensities are counts in arbitrary units after RescaleSlope; PPMI does not intensity-normalise, and papers report that "intra-population differences in dopamine transporter concentration and imperfect normalization are significant factors" [27] and that intensity normalisation matters more than spatial normalisation for CNNs [26]. (c) Some volumes are "misregistered" and were dropped by [25] (449 -> 365 PD). (d) The core-lab pipeline (HOSEM + Chang + 6 mm Gaussian + PMOD warp) is a different domain from the challenge's site-native reconstructions ("142 x 142 x 112 or 128 x 128 x 128", "2.46 mm and 3.895 mm isotropic") [17].

## 3. Data Use Agreement (v5.0, April 2026): the parts that matter to us [1]

- **Purpose limitation.** Access is granted "for the purpose of scientific investigation, teaching, or the planning of clinical research studies". The DrivenData organiser's reading: "competition use doesn't fall under those purposes" [19].
- **Cloud / Kaggle.** Clause 3: "I will not utilize AI tools, especially (but not exclusively) those with public-facing interfaces that do not offer guarantees regarding the containment of data inputs for PPMI data". Appendix A: analyses "must not use computational or AI resources owned by third parties who engage in the long-term retention of user content that is either shared or used in the training of public-facing models"; "An opt-out clause does not constitute sufficient protection"; "uploading PPMI data to a third-party platform that is not explicitly in compliance with the prohibition on sharing PPMI data is a clear violation of the DUA"; but "Investigators making use of computational resources such as Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Amazon Web Services, Runpod, or other computational services for the purposes of training AI models must take care to review the terms of use for the service to ensure that their workflow is secure". Kaggle's Terms grant Kaggle a "royalty-free, perpetual, irrevocable, and worldwide" licence to "reproduce, translate, modify, use, distribute, publicly display, publicly perform and otherwise act with respect to such User Submissions" [20], so PPMI data cannot go onto Kaggle (our current training path, see docs/how-to-run-on-a-second-kaggle-account.md). Local GPU is fine; a plain cloud VM is fine only after reading its terms. The Data Access Guidelines add that "All data will be stored at LONI or a PPMI-approved cloud service" [2].
- **Redistribution.** Clause 2: "I will not further disclose these data beyond the uses outlined in this agreement and my data use application." Clause 4: anyone "with whom I share these data" must "comply with this agreement by registering with the PPMI database". Appendix A: "PPMI prohibits sharing of participant-level data, even if it is processed or altered and lacks PPMI subject codes."
- **Sharing subject/scan lists with the organisers.** A PATNO/scan-ID list is participant-level data, so it may only go to people who have themselves registered with PPMI. DrivenData requires "proof (to DrivenData's satisfaction) of such license upon request" and a licence that permits use "without the need for additional approvals from any third party" [16]; prize eligibility needs external data that "is freely and publicly available to all Participants under a permissive open license that does not prohibit commercial use" [17][18]. Organiser ruling (mdaley, 2026-08-05): "models trained on this data wouldn't be eligible for prizes" [19]. Non-prize use was not explicitly addressed, but it still falls outside the DUA's purpose.
- **Publication / acknowledgement.** Clause 9: "sending manuscripts to the PPMI Data and Publications Committee (DPC) for administrative review" (external papers: "The DPC will have two weeks"; the DPC "does not review content") [3]. Clause 10 + policy: include "Data used in the preparation of this article was obtained on [YYY-MM-DD] from the Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative (PPMI) database (www.ppmi-info.org/access-data-specimens/download-data), RRID:SCR_006431" plus the funding-partners sentence; notify the DPC on publication; abstracts/posters go to the DPC too (clause 12: "also on a publicly accessible platform (e.g., Zenodo)"). Clause 11: PPMI "may request that I submit data generated from analyses"; clause 6: "annual requests to update this information".
- **Open-source model on PPMI + released weights.** "The use of PPMI data in training models is both permitted and encouraged, provided that the process is carried out in compliance with the DUA"; but "any model that has been trained using participant-level data may only be shared through MJFF-approved methods", and investigators "should consider whether the model could be used to easily reconstruct parts of the PPMI data set". IP policy 8(b): "No researcher or institution may claim any IP rights to any Study Materials or inventions arising out of the Study Materials". So: train yes; publish weights only with MJFF approval (resources@michaeljfox.org); an MIT release, as the challenge requires of winners, is not ours to grant unilaterally.

## 4. Access lead time

"Applications are reviewed by the Data and Publications Committee within one week of receipt" [4]; the FAQ says "most requests for access will be approved rapidly" with immediate access on approval [5]; images and the visual-read/SBR tables for PD/HC/SWEDD are Tier 1 ("Publicly available, refreshed daily"), while prodromal DaTscan results are Tier 3 ("within 30 days of receipt") [2][3]. Every team member who touches the data must hold their own account (clause 4). Applying today gives data around 2026-09-14 at best, two days before the deadline.

## 5. Recommended ingestion plan (research use only, off-Kaggle)

1. Apply at https://ida.loni.usc.edu/collaboration/access/appApply.jsp?project=PPMI with a research purpose (site-robust DaT classification), one account per team member; record the download date for the acknowledgement line.
2. In IDA: Download -> Image Collections -> Advanced Search, modality NM/SPECT, series "Reconstructed DaTSCAN"; Add to Collection -> Advanced Download with "As Archived" (DICOM) [29]; also grab the CSVs DaTscan Analysis, DaTscan Visual Interpretation Results, DaTscan Imaging, DaTscan Metadata, Participant_Status, Demographics. Check whether "Original" (site reconstruction / projections) series exist; if so, prefer them for anything meant to resemble the challenge's site-native scans.
3. Store on encrypted local disk only; never in the Kaggle dataset, shared drives, or artifacts_dev/.
4. Convert with pydicom -> SimpleITK, not dcm2niix: read the single multi-frame file, apply RescaleSlope, transpose (frames, rows, cols) -> (x, y, z), set spacing 2 mm, origin from IPP, identity direction (LPS), write .nii.gz. dcm2niix warns "Unable to determine slice direction: please check whether slices are flipped (derived image)" on workstation-derived NM RECON TOMO files with a single IPP and Slice Vector, and the maintainers treat such files as non-compliant rather than fixing them [21].
5. Verify orientation on the first ten volumes by overlaying the mean image on the MNI152 2 mm template (striatum centred, anterior at +y RAS, superior at +z); confirm the z-origin offset and that left/right are not mirrored (SimpleITK's LPS -> NIfTI handling does this correctly; a raw numpy dump does not).
6. Keep PPMI in its own frame: since the volumes are already in MNI 2 mm, skip our registration and apply at most a single affine MNI -> study-template; run our intensity step (hist mode) afterwards because PPMI intensities are raw counts.
7. Labels: use the per-scan visual-read table (positive = abnormal); fall back to cohort only for baseline PD (=1) / HC (=0); SWEDD baseline = 0; drop prodromal unless Tier 3 results are approved.
8. Splits by subject: group every EVENT_ID (SC, BL, V04, V06, V10, U*) of a PATNO into one fold; merge PATNO 4067 and 244087 (one person, two IDs [8]); follow-ups were co-registered to the screening scan, so they are near-duplicates.
9. QC: drop misregistered volumes (correlation with the cohort mean below a threshold, as in [25]); check RescaleSlope was applied; look at the count-scale distribution per site (DaTscan Imaging table).
10. Treat PPMI as one extra "site" in the DANN/DRO experiments to measure whether it helps site invariance; do not fold it into any challenge submission, and if a weights release is ever wanted, ask MJFF first.
